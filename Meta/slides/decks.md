# Decks

Central catalogue of locally authored presentation bundles found in `Projects/` and `Meta/`. There are **27 deck groups** below; source formats, drafts and PDF revisions are grouped rather than counted as separate talks. The new full-rerun deck was created and checked by this keeper. Older decks are included for discovery; their individual agent authorship, scientific claims and latest/approved revision were not re-established in this pass.

All PDF links refer to files currently present **on this laptop**. PDFs and PNGs remain gitignored; a fresh clone will not contain them. HTML/Markdown/TeX source links are retained. External reference-slide collections, paper/plot PDFs, speaker scripts alone, review notes alone and templates are not counted as authored decks. Archive/ was not scanned or edited.

Deck placement follows [[Meta/roles/keeper]]: a project without subprojects owns its deck; otherwise the actual leaf subproject owns it. This catalogue is not a claim that every leaf already has a keeper progress deck.
