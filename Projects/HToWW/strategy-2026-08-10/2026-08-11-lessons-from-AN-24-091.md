# What we're missing, per AN-24-091 (the Run 3 bbWW internal note)

`References/HToWW/AN2024_091_v11.pdf` — *Search for non-resonant Higgs boson pair production
in the HH → bb̄WW → bb̄2ℓ2ν decay channel with Run 3 data*, CMS AN-24-091 v11 (2026/02/09).
**√s = 13.6 TeV, 62.4 fb⁻¹, 2022+2023.**

This is the **internal note** behind arXiv:2604.02127, which
[[2026-08-11-mva-defined-regions-literature]] §4 identified as our closest Run 3 analogue.
Having the AN rather than the paper means we get the design *decisions*, not just the result.

**Why it maps onto us so well:** same era, same 13.6 TeV conditions, **dilepton WW final
state**, multiclass NN defining SRs *and* CRs by argmax, floating background rates,
autoMCStats — and, critically, they hit **the same MC-statistics wall we did** and wrote down
their solution.

---

## 1. ⭐ The binning rule — a principled fix for our dominant systematic

This is the single most actionable item. Their §7.1 (lines 410–414):

> "Before entering the final fit, a rebinning of the logit-transformed DNN output
> distributions is performed. In each category, **the binning is chosen such that the
> respective signal distribution is flat and that there are sufficiently many (≳ 3) effective
> MC background events present in each bin**:
>
> **σ_bkg < N_bkg / 3**"

Two mechanisms in one rule:

1. **A logit transform** of the NN output before binning. The raw softmax score piles up near
   0 and 1; the logit stretches those tails so a bin edge can actually resolve them.
2. **An explicit MC-statistics floor per bin** — the binning is *derived from* the background
   MC uncertainty, not fixed in advance.

**Why this matters for us.** Our SR has 10 fixed variable-width bins, and
[[2026-08-11-route-to-a-better-limit]] measured that MC stats costs us **252 units**
(1185 → 933 when frozen), concentrated in V+jets, whose per-bin errors run **14–23%**
(σ/N ≈ 0.14–0.23). Their rule demands **σ_bkg/N_bkg < 0.33** — which our vjets bins *do*
satisfy, so a naive application would not change our binning.

**The real lesson is the ordering**: they choose the binning **so that the constraint is
met**, adaptively per category. We fix 10 bins and then discover what the MC stats turn out
to be. Adopting their procedure would let the binning *respond* to the V+jets shortfall —
merging where vjets is weak, splitting where signal shape justifies it.

Note this reframes my earlier conclusion. I said "rebinning won't fix it" because only bin 1
falls below the autoMCStats threshold of 10 — that remains true for *removing nuisances*. But
their rule targets a different quantity (the relative error per bin, not the nuisance count),
and the flat-signal criterion is a sensitivity gain independent of MC stats. **Worth testing**,
with the honest expectation of a modest gain rather than the ~220 units only more MC can buy.

### They also tested the bin count directly (Table 15)

Asimov fits on 2022postEE alone (26.3 fb⁻¹), **rate + MC-stat uncertainties only**:

| bins (resolved) | expected limit σ/σ_theory |
|---|---:|
| 6 × 4 | 31 |
| 10 × 4 | 30 |
| **20 × 4** | **28** |

> "the expected sensitivity is rather robust against the choice of the DNN, the binning, and
> the categories"

**More bins helped slightly (30 → 28, ~7%)** even with MC-stat uncertainties included. This is
evidence *against* my earlier suggestion of coarsening the SR bins — for them, finer binning
won. Cheap to test on our side and it points the opposite way from where I was heading.

## 2. ⭐ CRs are yield-only — confirmed at AN level, with the exact bin counts

Table 10 gives the whole fit structure:

| category | 1b | ≥2b | boosted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| SR_ggF | 10 | 6 | 3 |
| SR_VBF | 8 | 6 | 3 |
| **tt** | **1** | **1** | — |
| **single-t** | **1** | **1** | — |
| **DY** | **1** | **1** | — |
| **single-H** | **1** | **1** | — |

> "**In the background CR, only the yield in each category enters the final fit.**"

**Every CR is literally 1 bin.** We fit a 10-bin shape in all five of our CRs. This is the
same recommendation as §3 of [[2026-08-10-analysis-strategy-from-AN]] (from AN-23-102 line
662), now with a **Run 3, argmax-defined, same-final-state** precedent. Given that our
residual JES/JER same-sign flags are channel-migration artifacts
([[2026-08-11-jes-jer-bug-fixed]]), collapsing the CRs to yields would remove that entire
class of artifact from five of six channels.

**Note the asymmetry**: SR bin counts *differ per category* (10 / 6 / 3), scaling with
available statistics. We use 10 everywhere.

## 3. Training weights explicitly chosen to minimise MC-stat uncertainty

Their §7.2 (lines 441–452) — a lever we have not touched:

> "**Background events in the NN_cat training:** the weighting is chosen such that each
> background process has the same number of effective events as the total effective number of
> signal events."
> "A **larger relative weight is assigned to the VV events in order to avoid large statistical
> fluctuations from few VV events in the signal bins.**"
> "This scheme has been chosen … to **minimize the size of MC statistical uncertainties**.
> Technically, the weighting is achieved not by event weights, but a **batch normalisation
> approach**."

They up-weight their *statistically weakest* background (VV) during training, specifically to
stop it fluctuating in the signal bins. **Our statistically weakest background is V+jets** —
the one costing 223 units in the SR. This is a training-time answer to a fit-time problem, and
it is orthogonal to simply generating more MC.

Caveat: acting on this means **retraining**, which is out of scope per your standing
instruction. Logged as an option, not a proposal.

## 4. Things that VALIDATE our current choices

Useful for convener defence:

| our choice | AN-24-091 |
|---|---|
| single `Total` JES source | **same** — "For the JEC uncertainty sources, **the 'total' source is chosen**", justified by impacts showing small effect and no strong constraints (lines 547–552). Contradicts AN-23-102's 11-source RegroupedV2 — **we are aligned with the newer Run 3 note.** |
| JES/JER uncorrelated across eras | same ("uncorrelated between the data-taking campaigns") |
| floating `tt` rate | same, and they go further — **four** floating rates |
| autoMCStats / Barlow–Beeston-lite | same (line 632) |
| argmax-defined SR + CRs | same |
| all eras trained inclusively | same — "All lepton-flavour channels and data-taking eras are treated inclusively both in the training and in the evaluation" |

**On JES in particular:** §5 of the strategy note flagged our single `Total` as a gap against
AN-23-102's 11 sources. AN-24-091 shows a Run 3 analysis making our choice deliberately, with
a stated justification. That downgrades the item from "gap" to "defensible decision", provided
we can show the same impacts argument.

## 5. Splitting floating normalisations by regime

> "**Two separate tt normalisation parameters** are assigned, one for the 1b and ≥2b
> categories and one for the boosted category, in order to account for potential modelling
> differences in the two very different pT regimes. For the DY background, **two separate
> normalisation parameters** … one to DY + HF and one to DY + LF, to account for
> flavour-dependent effects."

We have one `rate_params: [tt]`. Two ideas transfer:

- **Split tt by regime** if our categories span very different kinematics.
- **Split V+jets into HF and LF** — directly analogous to their DY+HF / DY+LF, and V+jets is
  precisely our problem process. A flavour-split floating normalisation would let the fit
  absorb HF/LF mismodelling instead of forcing one scale onto both.

Also worth noting: they apply **no** µR/µF or PDF rate uncertainty to tt and DY *because*
those normalisations float (lines 593–594). We apply `no_theory: [tt]` — the strategy note
listed this as an "open decision"; **AN-24-091 confirms it is the right one**, and suggests
extending it to any other process whose rate we float.

## 6. Partial unblinding as a background-model test

Their §9.1 (lines 635–638):

> "we perform fits to the classifier distributions but **blind the bins that have a noteworthy
> S/B**. The bins with S/√B > 0.008 in the ggF channels and S/√B > 0.004 in the VBF channels
> are blinded and excluded from the histogram. **This allows us to test the background model
> without unblinding the most sensitive bins.**"

A concrete recipe for validating the background model against data before full unblinding. We
have no equivalent step.

**None of items 1–3 requires retraining or reprocessing.** They are all config or
card-building changes, and they sit alongside — not instead of — the multi-era V+jets MC
increase, which remains the dominant lever (1185 → 1037 measured).

**One honest correction:** in [[2026-08-11-route-to-a-better-limit]] I suggested coarser SR
binning might help. Their Table 15 shows finer binning helping slightly (30 → 28 going 10×4 →
20×4) even with MC-stat uncertainties on. Test both directions rather than assuming coarser
is better.
