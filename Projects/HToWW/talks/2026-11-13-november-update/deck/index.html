The H+c analysis in Run 3

H → WW → eμνν · four eras · 61.9 fb⁻¹

A rare signal in a large background

  • One opposite-flavour eμ pair, missing transverse momentum > 45 GeV, and a c-jet candidate.
  • A six-class event classifier separates H+c, Higgs backgrounds, tt, single top, diboson and V+jets.
  • Each event enters the signal region (SR) or one of five control regions (CRs).
H + c → eμ + pTmiss + c

What changes in this round?

  • Two descriptions of the signal: central 4FS and stitched 3FS/4FS.
  • New prompt-lepton selection and retrained event classifiers.
  • A future full-systematics fit with two SRs: Nc-jet = 1 and ≥ 2.

The analysis is being rebuilt around the updated objects and signal samples.
