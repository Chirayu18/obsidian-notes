# Indico 1716863: relevance to current research

**Agent-reported survey, checked against Indico and the vault on 2026-11-05.** Recommendations are inferences, not validation of the analysis or of speakers' scientific claims. This adds a cross-project survey to [[Projects/CombineAgent/evaluations/2026-10-21-kb-agents-workshop-takeaways|the earlier CombineAgent workshop note]]; its detailed historical claims are not all revalidated here.

## Event

**Knowledge Bases and Agents for CERN**, CERN, **19–23 October 2026**, Europe/Zurich. It has already finished. The overview's date envelope is 19 October 09:00 to 23 October 21:00; the actual timetable starts at 10:00 on Monday. Purpose: compare knowledge-base and agent deployments across experiments, identify shared needs, and develop common infrastructure and collaborations. Sources: [overview](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1716863/overview), [timetable](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1716863/timetable/), [contribution list](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1716863/contributions/).

The overview lists these **chairpersons** (the export has no separate organiser text): Ben Couturier, Christoph Paus, Dmytro Kovalskyi, Hasan Ozturk, Jason Mohoney, Jason Oliver, Joaquin Hoya, Josh Bendavid, Luca Lavezzo, Mariarosaria D'Alfonso and Pietro Lugato. The overview describes organisation by CERN and experiment members from management and technical teams.

## Current-work evidence

- **HToWW Run A/B:** [[Projects/HToWW/samples-and-production/full-rerun/2026-11-05-fullfix-rerun-run-a-state|5 November full-fix handoff]], including its latest progress sections. The record now says NNLO normalisation and Thomas's lepton-MVA port are implemented, with a local baseline commit and smoke tests; Run A is held by EOS quota. Run B uses the official zero/at-least-one charm-jet split, with radius and eta choices recorded as Chirayu's decisions. The event cannot settle the 3FS/4FS ratio, scale-weight/Yukawa correction, WH cross sections, NNLO conversion or stitching definitions.
- **Lepton MVA and speed:** the same handoff records ONNX/TMVA comparison, open SF provenance/factorisation questions, and event-MVA retraining before limits. [[Projects/HToWW/framework/optimization/2026-11-05-speedup-patches|Speed-up proposals]] initially reported output-equivalence checks on copied trees, a nondeterministic baseline from unseeded smearing, and unapplied patches. **Later 2026-11-05:** patches 01–04 were reported applied in `40d0e3e` and checked by the [[Projects/HToWW/2026-11-05-sol-review-unpushed-work|independent review]]; patch 05 remains undecided. Those are local evidence claims, not workshop results.
- **CombineAgent/limits:** [[Projects/CombineAgent/evaluations/2026-10-21-omp-qwen-baseline|omp/Qwen baseline]] and [[Projects/CombineAgent/evaluations/2026-10-21-kb-agents-workshop-takeaways|existing workshop evaluation note]] document real public questions, repeated trials, nugget scoring, wrong answers and timeouts. HToWW inference also needs closure of the actual statistical model.
- **Flashjet:** [[Projects/Flashjet/kappa-hce/2026-11-02-kappa-hce-rescue|kappa-HCE rescue]] includes the later training-balance amendment and interim results; the preregistered rescue verdict remains pending in that record. Preserve its fixed validation/test protocol.
- **DigiMorphing/Alpaka:** [[Projects/DigiMorphing/phase2-morphing/2026-11-02-cmssw-pr-draft|Phase-2 clusterizer PR draft]] distinguishes implementation/output checks from still-needed physics validation and timing. [[Projects/Alpaka/AlpakaFollowUp|Alpaka follow-up]] itself contains little recent technical status.
- **Agenthon and research memory:** [[Projects/Agenthon/2026-11-03-preprint-report|preprint report]], including the later concurrent-poster update, leaves novelty/scope and controlled benchmarking unresolved. [[Daily/2026-11-03|recent daily record]] documents workflow reliability and evidence-status problems. Current vault instructions establish git-backed notes, a single worker, keeper review and retired Hindsight; older harness records are historical.
