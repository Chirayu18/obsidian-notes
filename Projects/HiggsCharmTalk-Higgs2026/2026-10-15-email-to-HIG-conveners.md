# Draft email to HIG conveners (not sent)

To: Mauro.Donega@cern.ch, meng.xiao@cern.ch
Cc: matthias.schroeder@uni-hamburg.de
Subject: Higgs 2026 talk "Constraints on Higgs-charm couplings": planned content

Dear Mauro, dear Meng,

I am giving the CMS talk "Constraints on Higgs-charm couplings" at Higgs 2026 (28 October, 15+5 min), and I would like to check the planned content with you before the rehearsal on 19 October.

The talk uses public results only. For direct searches I plan to show VH(H→cc) (HIG-21-008, PRL 131 (2023) 061801), ttH(H→cc) together with its combination with VH (HIG-24-018, PRL 136 (2026) 011801), which gives the headline |κc| < 3.5 (2.7 expected), and the boosted H→cc search (HIG-21-012, PRL 131 (2023) 041801). For charm associated production I have a single slide covering cH(γγ) (HIG-23-010, JHEP 11 (2025) 060) and cH(WW) with the γγ+WW combination (HIG-24-009, JHEP 08 (2026) 051), and I point to the H+c flavour scheme talk that follows mine and to the rare Higgs talks for details.

For indirect constraints I include the κc interpretations of the Higgs pT spectra (HIG-17-028, HIG-21-009 and the 2D κb–κc contours of HIG-23-013, JHEP 04 (2026) 093), the γH plus H→4ℓ light Yukawa result (HIG-23-011, PRD 112 (2025) 112001), the off-shell plus on-shell H→4ℓ result (HIG-25-003, arXiv:2607.23352) and H→J/ψγ (SMP-22-012, PLB 865 (2025) 139462), followed by one summary table of all κc constraints with their assumptions.

Since no Run 3 Higgs-charm result is public yet, the Run 3 slide only shows public tools and inputs: the HLT flavour tagging paper (BTV-25-002, arXiv:2608.18331), the tt+b/c cross sections (CMS-PAS-TOP-26-003), |Vcb| at 13.6 TeV with UParT (CMS-PAS-TOP-26-002) and the UParT performance note (CMS-DP-2025-081). For HL-LHC prospects I quote the joint ATLAS+CMS projection |κc| < 1.5 (HIG-25-002, arXiv:2504.00672) and show the HIG-21-008 supplementary Phase-2 κb–κc contour.

Could you tell me whether this list is fine, or whether I should add anything? In particular, is any Run 3 Higgs-charm result (for example the boosted VH(bb/cc) analysis) expected to become public before 28 October, so that I could include it? I would also welcome any preference on how much to show on H+c and on the indirect probes, given the other talks in the programme.

I will upload the draft slides to CINCO by 19 October.

Best regards,
Chirayu
